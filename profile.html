Tracey has worked in a range of leadership and executive roles throughout her career. She has worked as a senior Ministerial Advisor to both state and federal ministers and members of Parliament. Tracey has led government policy reform leading social and public policy innovation.

Tracey comes from a family of business owners with her parents being the co-founders and owners of Alpha Electrics and Alpha Irrigation. This business has recently celebrated 50 years of success. As the CEO of Amovita, Tracey leads the Global Strategy Division with a career over 25 years. She has provided lectureship at the Australian Catholic University in Brisbane for eight years where she lectured in the fields of public and social policy, leadership and organisational excellence.

With an interest in research on high-performance leadership and neuroscience, Tracey has developed an integrated and neurological approach to coaching and supervision through Amovita’s supervision and coaching models. Tracey is currently a PhD Candidate with Griffith University in Brisbane researching supervisor capability and leadership. Tracey is an international Author and Speaker on business and organisational excellence.

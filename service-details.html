EAP Services

Amovita’s EAP services are uniquely designed so your staff see the same practitioner throughout the entire program — not a roster system. Because consistency builds real outcomes.

Talk to us Download EAP Info Pack

A Workplace Employee Support Program that goes further

Our EAP services are unique and achieve great results. Our professional team is not placed on a roster system — your staff can see the same person for the duration of the service.

After EAP support ends, staff can continue care through a Mental Health Care Plan with referrals to specialist counselling and treatment. Amovita also supports leaders and HR teams with complex workplace issues, offering tailored wellbeing and consulting programs.

What’s included

Not a roster — your staff see the same qualified practitioner every time

Specialist referrals and a full range of counselling and therapy options

Post-EAP access to a Mental Health Care Plan for continuity of care

HR advisory support for complex workplace situations

The Exceler8 Framework

With 30 years experience in consulting services, our EAP services focus on any challenge staff face in every day life. We develop and deliver a full range of wellbeing and lifestyle counselling services to ensure your staff feel supported. We provide counselling services in your workplace, off site or outreach where required. We offer counselling services in person, through our online telehealth service and via phone.

Our counselling approaches integrate a developmental approach so staff are able to integrate what is focused on in their sessions back into the workplace and their life. This assists to promote resilience and lasting outcomes. Our specialist EAP services include: industrial support, human resource advisory services, return to work programs, injury management, performance assessments, training on working with different personality conditions in the workplace, rehabilitation host placements, transition support services.

Our Exceler8 Framework includes generalist counselling services, a managers referral and support service, mobile counselling services, information sessions, emergency response and critical incident service, pandemic response, regional and remote workplace support, workplace investigations and mediations, team reviews and performance management.
